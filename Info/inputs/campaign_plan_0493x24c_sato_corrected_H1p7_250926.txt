Article assertion addressed
---------------------------
Determine whether the large deficit and lack of stationarity previously observed
at H/D=1.7 were materially contaminated by the historical geometry change in
which the nozzle was shortened when the stand-off was increased.

The x24c case keeps exactly the x24b nozzle geometry (20-cell throat, 40-cell
length, 4-cell walls) and changes only its vertical position relative to the bath,
so that H/D changes from 0.8 to 1.7 by rigid translation.  Brinkman walls are
retained, with alphaMax=8e5 as qualified by the x24b anchor.

Decision contract
-----------------
1. If h/D increases substantially and/or the 3000:4000 stability improves relative
   to historical x14at H/D=1.7, the historical nozzle-length change is identified
   as a material confounder.  Proceed to a small corrected H/D campaign.
2. If x24c remains close to the historical H/D=1.7 response, do not attribute the
   deficit primarily to geometry.  The next investigation must focus on physical
   similarity (rhoG/rhoL, ReG, ReL, Oh, jet development and gas-pressure state).

   repeated reruns are used to manufacture agreement.
